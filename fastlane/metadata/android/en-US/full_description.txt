EarLoft is an audio player for your own collection: music, podcasts and books. It plays the files on your phone together with your self-hosted music server (Navidrome, Jellyfin or Emby) and your Audiobookshelf server in one app – on your phone, on your Wear OS watch and in Android Auto. Podcasts come straight from their RSS feeds. No streaming subscription, no account with a third party.

<b>Music</b>
• One library from local files and your servers, without duplicates
• Gapless playback, ReplayGain and synchronized lyrics
• Mixes of similar songs, and “Keep playing” so the music never stops
• Favorites, playlists and play counts sync with your server
• Automatic downloads of your favorite, newest and most played albums

<b>Podcasts</b>
• Search Apple Podcasts and Podcast Index, or add any RSS or Atom feed
• Chapters, show notes with timestamps, transcripts
• Speed, shorter silences, volume boost, skip intro and outro – per podcast
• Automatic downloads and an editable “Continue listening” queue
• OPML import and export

<b>Audiobooks</b>
• Your Audiobookshelf libraries and the audiobooks on your phone
• Progress and bookmarks sync both ways, also after listening offline
• Chapters and adjustable jumps
• Automatic download of the next book in a series

<b>Everywhere</b>
• Download everything for offline listening
• Sleep timer – for podcasts and audiobooks, shaking your phone extends the sleep timer
• Wear OS app: music, podcasts and books on your watch without your phone, with Bluetooth headphones
• Android Auto, Chromecast and home screen widgets
• Material You design, light and dark, in 14 languages

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, no tracking, no analytics. EarLoft is open source (GPLv3). Your library and listening history stay on your device and your own servers.
